Branches of GeographyPhysical Geography:Geomorphology: Relates to the Lithosphere (Land).  Climatology: Relates to the Atmosphere (Air).  Oceanography: Relates to the Hydrosphere (Water).  Other Components: Biosphere.  Detailed Syllabus Breakdown1. GeomorphologyUniverse and Cosmology.  Latitude and longitude.  Interior of the earth (including body waves like P and S waves, epicenter, focus, and focal depth).  Formation of the continent.  Plate tectonism.  Geomorphic processes (Endogenic and exogenic).  Earthquake, Tsunami, and Volcanism.  2. ClimatologyComposition and Structure of Atmosphere.  Insolation and Heat Budget.  Temperature.  Atmospheric circulation and Pressure belts.  Winds (Primary, secondary, and tertiary winds).  Clouds and rainfall.  Cyclones.  3. OceanographyImportance of the Ocean.  Water index and Deep sea plain.  Ocean temperature and Ocean salinity.  Ocean currents and Ocean tides.  Marine resource and Maritime law.  4. Indian GeographyGeneral introduction and Physiography of India.  Drainage, Climate, and seasons of India.  Vegetation, Soil, and Agriculture of India.  Minerals and Industries.  Transport and Population.  5. World GeographyContinents covered: Asia, Africa, Europe, North America, South America, Australia, Antarctica.
